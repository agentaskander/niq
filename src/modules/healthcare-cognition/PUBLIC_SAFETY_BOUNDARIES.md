# Public Safety Boundaries

This module is not a medical device, clinical decision support tool, diagnostic system, or treatment recommender.

## Allowed

- Synthetic workflow examples
- Public-safe healthcare operations terminology
- Non-clinical continuity and handoff concepts
- Demo-only provenance and review indicators

## Not Allowed

- PHI
- Diagnosis
- Treatment instructions
- Medication recommendations
- Private scoring formulas
- Credentials or environment variables
- Production workflow claims
