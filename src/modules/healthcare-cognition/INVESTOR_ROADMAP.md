# Investor Roadmap

## Phase 1: Public Concept

Synthetic lab demonstrating healthcare cognition surfaces, workflow continuity, and review boundaries.

## Phase 2: Pilot Readiness

Governed pilot workflows with explicit compliance review, non-production data controls, and clinician-facing feedback.

## Phase 3: Integration Readiness

EHR-adjacent export patterns, audit trails, and role-specific documentation surfaces.

## Phase 4: Platform Expansion

Specialty packages, governance dashboards, and partner-facing workflow intelligence.
