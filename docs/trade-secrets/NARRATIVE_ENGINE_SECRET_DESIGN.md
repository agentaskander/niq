# Narrative Engine Secret Design

Confidential internal material.

Protect the deterministic and future AI-backed narrative transformation system:

- symptom-to-story mapping
- timeline ordering logic
- narrative assembly rules
- role-aware language controls
- safe phrase replacements
- unsupported diagnosis and treatment-order filters
- style transformations for nursing, advanced clinical, provider progress note, SOAP, SBAR, handoff, triage, and telehealth

The engine should use only supplied facts, preserve clinician review, and keep treatment decisions outside autonomous generation. Internal narrative templates and prompt clauses are trade-secret assets and should not be published.
