# NarrativeIQ Trade Secret Strategy

Confidential internal material. Do not expose publicly, include in marketing pages, publish in sales collateral, or share outside approved company channels.

NarrativeIQ's defensible advantage is the Clinical Story Workflow Dataset and the systems that convert clinician workflow behavior into safer, faster documentation experiences.

Protected assets include:

- Clinical Story Engine mapping logic
- Specialty ontology and prompt-safe phrase libraries
- Narrative transformation rules by role and mode
- Workflow event logs and usage sequences
- Narrative acceptance, edit, copy, and rejection patterns
- Adoption dataset and beta workflow feedback

Public materials may describe NarrativeIQ as learning from de-identified workflow patterns. Public materials must not disclose internal scoring formulas, ontology details beyond demo-safe subsets, prompt/template internals, or workflow intelligence dashboards.
